Thesis: The data story becomes the bulletin board on a pantry's door, where every pantry's hours sheet is taped up and the corrections are written on in marker; it refuses the data-journalism article page (serif headline, stat row, card figures) the last two projects shipped. First screen: Kraft board ground. Left column: a typed notice sheet taped at the top (the offer line as the headline, two lines of dek with the 95 / 54 / 50 figures highlighted), and under it three small hours sheets taped at slight angles: Weekdays 8-5 · 53 open, Evenings after 5 · 17 open, Saturday · 12 open. They are the switch (real buttons, aria-pressed); the chosen one straightens, gets a pushpin and a highlighter stroke. Right column: the live tract map printed on a large sheet pinned to the board, its layer and rider options as tick-boxes along the sheet's top edge, and a yellow sticky note overlapping its lower corner that holds the tapped tract or pantry. Phone: notice, the three sheets in one row, the map sheet, the sticky note.
Type and color: Rendered Public Sans + Permanent Marker against Atkinson Hyperlegible + Kalam at 1440 and 390. Public Sans won: its Black weight reads as a flyer typed in a church office, and Permanent Marker reads as an actual Sharpie; Atkinson's slashed zero looked wrong in prose figures (30, 50%) and Kalam read as notebook doodling. Ground kraft #b99b72, paper #fdfcf8, ink #1a1714, marker red #c42b1c for corrections only, highlighter yellow as the one accent (selection and 'open in this window'). Light: it's read in offices and on phones in daylight; dark mode only darkens the board, the paper stays paper.
Own system: Surfaces are paper on board, nothing else: every container is a sheet with square corners, a two-part paper shadow (contact + drop) and one fastener (tape, pushpin, staple or clipboard clip), never a border box. Sheets sit at small deterministic rotations (−2° to 2°) and straighten when active. Typed voice = Public Sans (400 body, 700 labels, 900 headings); handwritten voice = Permanent Marker, red, only for corrections and conditions taken from the listings (2nd & 4th Sat only, call first, residents only, holiday closures). Highlighter = selection. Map ramp = ballpoint-blue ordinal ramp (validated); open pantries highlighter-filled with ink ring, closed ones dashed.
